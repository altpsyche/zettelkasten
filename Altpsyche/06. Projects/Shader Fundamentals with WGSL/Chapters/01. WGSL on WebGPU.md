## Beats

**Intro: why WebGPU exists**
1. One portable API. OpenGL, 1992, ran everywhere.
2. It stopped fitting the hardware. Vulkan, Metal and DirectX 12 each replaced it on one platform.
3. That bought speed and cost portability. Four modern APIs, none of them everywhere.
4. WebGPU is the portable layer back on top of Vulkan, Metal and DirectX.
5. So it is not a new low level API. It wraps the three that already exist.
6. What WebGL never had: compute shaders, indirect drawing, render bundles, external textures, flexible canvas integration, better debugging.
7. And no global state, which was WebGL's main design flaw.
8. Of that list this series uses compute. The rest is renderer work.
9. Not browser only. Browsers through JavaScript, TypeScript and WASM. Node. Native through Dawn for C and C++, wgpu for Rust. Community bindings past that.

**For GLSL readers**
10. What carries over unchanged, and what does not.
11. The remap they need: types, entry points, bindings, no global state.

**What WGSL is**
12. One language, three stages: vertex, fragment, compute.
13. This series uses fragment and compute. No vertex stage, no textures.
14. Where the code will live. The playground, nothing to install.

**Close**
15. What chapter 2 starts.
