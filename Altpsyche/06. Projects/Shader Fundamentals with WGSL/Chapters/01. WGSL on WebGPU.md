## Beats

**Intro: why WebGPU exists**
1. One portable API. OpenGL, 1992, ran everywhere.
2. It stopped fitting the hardware. Vulkan, Metal and DirectX 12 each replaced it on one platform.
3. That bought speed and cost portability. Four modern APIs, none of them everywhere.
4. WebGPU is the portable layer back on top of Vulkan, Metal and DirectX.
5. So it is not a new low level API. It wraps the three that already exist.
6. Over WebGL it adds compute and drops global state.
7. Not browser only either. Dawn for C++, wgpu for Rust.

**For GLSL readers**
8. What carries over unchanged, and what does not.
9. The remap they need: types, entry points, bindings, no global state.
